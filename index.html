Lunacia Tactical Combat Rules

⭐ Non-Atomic Turns

Unlike chess or Fire Emblem, your actions do not end your turn. You have AP for spells and MP for movement. You can move 1 tile, attack, move 2 more tiles, and attack again as long as you have points remaining.

💥 Push and Collision Mechanics

Abilities like Knockback Arrow or Tackle push the target. If the target hits an obstacle (rock/tree) or another unit, they stop and both take bonus collision damage!

🎮 Keyboard Controls & Camera Handling

  • WASD / Directional Arrow Keys: Pan camera freely across the battlefield (movement relative to screen).
  • Q / E: Rotate isometric perspective 45° to the left or right.
  • R: Center and focus camera instantly on the active hero.
  • Mouse Wheel (Scroll): Dynamic zoom in and out.
  • Right Click + Drag: Free 3D orbital view rotation and pitch / elevation.
  • Middle Click (Wheel) + Drag: Pan and drag tactical terrain.
  • Left Click on Tile: Move to tile or cast selected spell.
  • 1, 2, 3: Select Spell 1, 2, or 3.
  • Escape: Cancel spell selection (return to movement mode).
  • Space: End your current turn.
